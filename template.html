Shot-Quality Decay

Does a player's own shot selection change the longer they've been in the game? Plus the full rotation-decay breakdown by stint length, below.

Does shot quality decay? (the headline finding)

Net rating is confounded by the other 9 players on the floor -- that's why every cut of it below kept reducing to "coaches sub players out when things go wrong." A player's own shot selection isn't confounded that way: eFG% and average shot distance, by how long the shooter had been in their current stint when they took it. Team-wide it's flat (no decay -- confirming the net-rating pattern really was mostly noise), but individually there's real signal.
