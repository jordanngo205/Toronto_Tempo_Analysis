Shot-Quality Decay

Does a player's own shot selection change the longer they've been in the game? Plus the full rotation-decay breakdown by stint length, below.

Does shot quality decay? (the headline finding)

Net rating is confounded by the other 9 players on the floor -- that's why every cut of it below kept reducing to "coaches sub players out when things go wrong." A player's own shot selection isn't confounded that way: eFG% and average shot distance, by how long the shooter had been in their current stint when they took it. Team-wide it's flat (no decay -- confirming the net-rating pattern really was mostly noise), but individually there's real signal.

Where exactly does each player's performance fall off?

The 3-bucket view compares whole-stint averages -- it can't say when within a stint someone turns. This slices every stint minute-by-minute instead (minute 0-1 of the stint, 1-2, 2-3, ...), pooling across every stint that lasted that long, then subtracts the team's own rate at that same minute (leave-one-out) -- so a player's own cliff point survives the adjustment instead of getting smeared into one number. Line above zero = better than a teammate would be at that point in a stint; below zero = worse. Faded/dashed segments are under 10 stints reaching that minute -- read those as noise, not signal.
