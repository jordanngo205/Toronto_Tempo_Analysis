Shot-Quality & Cliff-Point Decay

Which players' performance actually changes the longer they've been in the game, and where -- plus which 5-man lineups have actually worked this season.

Does shot quality decay? (the headline finding)

A player's own shot selection isn't confounded by the other 9 players on the floor the way net rating is: eFG% and average shot distance, by how long the shooter had been in their current stint when they took it. Team-wide it's flat (no decay), but individually there's real signal.

Where exactly does each player's performance fall off?

Every stint sliced minute-by-minute (minute 0-1, 1-2, 2-3, ...), pooling across every stint that lasted that long, then subtracting the team's own rate at that same minute (leave-one-out) -- so a player's own cliff point survives the adjustment instead of getting smeared into one number. Line above zero = better than a teammate would be at that point in a stint; below zero = worse. Faded/dashed segments are under 10 stints reaching that minute -- read those as noise, not signal.
