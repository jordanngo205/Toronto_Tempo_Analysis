Does shot quality decay? (a cleaner signal than net rating)

Net rating above is confounded by the other 9 players on the floor -- that's why it kept reducing to "coaches sub players out when things go wrong." A player's own shot selection isn't confounded that way. eFG% and average shot distance, by how long the shooter had been in their current stint when they took it.
